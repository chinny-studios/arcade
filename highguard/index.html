          How to command

          W S
          Throttle: astern, all stop, one quarter, half, three quarters, full ahead
          A D · R F
          Turn to port or starboard · climb or dive. Capital ships are heavy: start turns early
          Mouse · wheel
          Look around the ship · camera distance. The sight in the centre of the screen is where your guns aim
          Left click (hold)
          Fire every battery that bears on the sight: port and starboard broadsides, and the bow guns straight ahead
          Right click
          Zoom in for long shots
          Space
          Torpedo salvo at your target (heavy cruisers and battlecruisers; the target must be ahead)
          Tab · T
          Next enemy warship · the ship under your sight (allies too)
          Q E
          Pick a subsystem on the target: bridge, engines, shield generator, batteries, hangar
          1 2 3 4
          Reinforce fore, aft, port or starboard shields (press again to balance them)
          Z X C
          Your squadrons. Tap: launch, or attack your target (escort it, if it is a friend). Shift+tap: escort us. Hold: recall to rearm
          V · Esc
          Every squadron attacks your target · pause
          Broadsides: most of your guns point sideways. Turn so the target sits on your beam, aim at the yellow diamond (it leads the target), and fire. Shields have four facings. Fire on one facing until it drops and your bolts reach the hull and the systems behind it. Put your strongest shield toward the enemy. Subsystems: a ship without engines crawls, without a shield generator its shields bleed away, without a battery that side goes quiet, without a hangar it can't launch, and without a bridge it can barely aim. Your damage-control crews bring your own systems back after a while. Fighters screen against bombers; bombers carry torpedoes that punch through half a shield. Flak guns fire on their own.
