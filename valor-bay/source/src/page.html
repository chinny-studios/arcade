Controls

Crimes break out all over the city: red markers on the radar and the screen edges. Stop them for points; leave them and the city slides toward chaos, and supervillains come out to play. Punching lunges to the nearest crook, so keep attacking and push toward the next one to chain combos. A "!" means a crook is about to swing or shoot: dodge it.
