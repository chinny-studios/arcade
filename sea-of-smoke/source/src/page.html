Win by reaching 1000 points, sinking the enemy fleet, or leading when the clock runs out. Held points add to your score; a ship lost or taken costs her fleet dearly. Lead your shots: balls take seconds to arrive, so aim where a moving ship will be (the yellow diamond helps). Stay hidden: ships are seen at their concealment range, much farther just after firing. Fog banks, the squall and smoke screens hide you. Forts fire for whoever holds their point. Silence an enemy fort with round shot.
