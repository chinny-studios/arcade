Act of God

You are a god

The city below goes about its day. Pick a power from Wrath or Grace, set its size, and use it. The people react: they flee, fire engines and ambulances race to help, and the news reports everything you do.

  • Left click to use a power. Some need holding: Lightning, Wildfire, Deluge, Heal, Restore, Prosper, Bloom, Raise land.
  • Drag across water with Part the waters.
  • Right drag to turn, wheel to zoom, drag with no power (or Shift) to move.
  • 1–0, Z–B powers, Tab wrath or grace
  • [ ] size, Esc put the power down
  • WASD move, Q E turn, R F zoom
  • Space pause, T speed, N time of day, H hide the interface
  • Tap or hold to use a power. Drag across water with Part the waters.
  • Two fingers move, pinch to zoom and twist to turn.
  • Tap ✋ to put the power down and drag the view with one finger.

Grace can mend most of what wrath does. Restore raises ruins and heals the land; only Resurrection brings back the dead.
