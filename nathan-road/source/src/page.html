Controls

Keyboard and mouse: WASD to walk, mouse to look, Shift to run, E or click to use and talk, C for the camera (click or Space to take a photo), M for the map, J for the notebook, T to wait an hour, Esc to pause.

Touch: left stick to walk, drag anywhere to look, USE, RUN and CAM on the right, MAP and BOOK at the top.

Gamepad: left stick to walk, right stick to look, A to use, X for the camera, click the left stick or hold RT to run, Y for the map, Back for the notebook, Start to pause.

About this map

Streets, buildings, trees, bus stops, crossings and shop positions come from Overture Maps (2026-10-21 release), which is built from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and other open sources. Hills come from the AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen). Distances are true to scale: one metre in the game is one metre in Tsim Sha Tsui.

Shop signs carry the real names from the Overture places listings (from Meta, Microsoft and others), placed on the shopfront facing the street in each listing's address. Milton Mansion's directory and door plates list the tenants in those listings. Listings go out of date and some positions are approximate, so expect the odd shop that has moved or closed. Buildings carry their mapped names; where the data has no height, a building gets a plausible one, and facades are generic textures.

The people you meet, their stalls and their errands are made up. Landmark notes are short and simplified.

The MTR: Tsim Sha Tsui, Jordan, East Tsim Sha Tsui and Austin stations sit under their real spots, and their exits carry their real letters and the streets they serve. Where each exit opens on the pavement, the station interiors, train times and fares are approximate. Stations past the edge of the map aren't built yet: ride there and you come straight back.

Sign lettering uses Noto Sans TC (SIL Open Font License). Engine: three.js. All sound is synthesized.
