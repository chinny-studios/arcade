A BRINDLE ISLE ADVENTUREThe Keeper's
Errand

Nine days of storm, and tonight the lighthouse went dark. A pompous sky-baron has "bought the weather". Take your grandmother's tide-pebble, mind your moods, and bring the light home.

Original game. Music and sound are synthesized in your browser.

How to Play

Move WASD or arrows (left stick)   Talk / use E (B)
Mode action Space or right-click (A)   Throw pebble F or left-click (X, RT)
Change mode 1–4, Tab or Q (Y, LB/RB)   Tonic H (LT)   Map M   Pause Esc
Normal: talk, search, push crates, turn valves. Throws a medium arc.
Sporty: run fast and jump with Space. Throws a high lob.
Aggressive: punch, punch, kick with Space. Throws hard and flat. People won't talk to you like this.
Sneaky: tiptoe silently; bailiffs only see you up close and in front. Throws a low skim.
The pebble bounces off walls and always comes home. Fall in the sea or a pit and you lose a little life. Smash crates for clams; buy kelp tonic in Pottle Harbour.

Options

Paused

M or Esc to close. Gold stars mark where to go next.

The Light Returns

The Storm Engine is scrap, the Baron has flown off in a huff, and for the first time in nine days the sun is shining on Brindle Isle. Wick's lamp turns again over the harbour.

But somewhere above the clouds, a gilded airship is drafting a very large bill...
