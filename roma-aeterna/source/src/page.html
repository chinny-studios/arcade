Roma Aeterna

Rome, in the ninth year of Hadrian · AD 125

The emperor is home from Greece. Walk the city as it stood: the Forum and the Capitol, the Colosseum and the Circus, the new Pantheon, and the loud, crowded streets of the Subura where Rome actually lived. Then take the Via Appia out past the tombs to the little town of Bovillae.
