How to play

Survive. Eat up to three foods at once: each raises your maximum health and stamina for a while. Rest by a fire beneath a roof to become Rested. Rain makes you Wet and night makes you Cold unless you're near a fire.

Progress. Pick up branches, stones and flint. Craft a hammer by hand, build a workbench under a roof, then tools, a bow and armour. Calm Shirokiba, the white boar god, to earn a pickaxe for copper and tin. Smelt bronze, then cross the water on a raft to wake Ōsugi, the elder cedar.
