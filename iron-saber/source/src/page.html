IRON SABER

Mobile suit operations · New Haven front
BUILDING THE WORLD…
You start on foot at the Coalition base. Walk to a kneeling suit and press E to ride the winch up into its cockpit. Every suit stands to scale: an 18 m machine beside 6 m houses and 250 m towers.
WASD move · Mouse look · Shift run / boost dash · Space jump / hold to thrust · LMB fire · RMB shield · Q vulcans · R missiles · F saber · E board / exit · C call your suit · V first / third person · M map · N mute · Esc pause
